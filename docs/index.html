How it works

  1. Install. Open the download, drag Dustpan to Applications, open it. A thin divider and a chevron appear in your menu bar.
  2. Hold ⌘ and drag the icons you don't need to the left of the divider. macOS lets you reorder any menu bar icon this way.
  3. Click the chevron. The divider stretches to 10,000 points wide and shoves everything on its left off the edge of the screen. Click again and it shrinks back.

That really is the whole trick. The apps behind the hidden icons keep running normally, and icons to the right of the divider, including Apple's own, are never touched. Dustpan needs no Accessibility or Screen Recording permission because it never looks at anything; it only resizes its own divider. The code is open for you to check.
